This template outlines a subscription service for professional writing services. The service offers access to a team of professional writers who will provide high-quality written content on a regular basis. There are three plans to choose from, each with different recurring prices and intervals. The plans include a Basic plan for occasional written content, a Pro plan for regular written content, and a Premium plan for a high volume of written content. The template also includes options for a trial period and the ability to assign digital product files for subscribers. The template does not specify how the subscription service will be offered, and it is for you to use it as a guide for creating your own service.
