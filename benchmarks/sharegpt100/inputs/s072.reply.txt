Here is a revised version of the comic that is simpler and funnier:

Panel 1: A group of woodland creatures are sitting at a table for their book club meeting. There is a knock at the door.
Pink: Who's that?
Red: Maybe a new member for our book club!

Panel 2: Red panics as she sees a dragon licking its lips behind the curtain.
Red: It's a dragon! Run for your lives everyone!

Panel 3: The dragon, who is a mom and looks apologetic, says "I'm not here to eat anyone. I heard you had a book club?"
Red: Uh, yes.

Panel 4: The dragon looks excited as she holds up her favorite book. Pink looks interested, but Red looks grossed out.
Dragon: Awesome, it's nice to meet you! I brought my favorite book too!
Pink: What a lovely book!
Red: Ugh, I'll pass on reading that.
