The nerve that is responsible for the sensation to the palmar surface of the 5th digit or little finger is the ulnar nerve. Damage to the ulnar nerve, particularly at the level of the wrist or elbow, can cause numbness or tingling in the little finger and half of the ring finger on the palmar side. This condition is known as ulnar nerve neuropathy or ulnar nerve entrapment. It can also cause weakness in the hand, particularly in the muscles that control finger movements, leading to difficulty with gripping or manipulating objects.
